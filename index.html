📖 Atmospheric Dispersion Science & CPCB AQI Standards

Transparent mathematical and regulatory framework for air quality assessment

The Indian National Air Quality Index (AQI) is defined by the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change. The sub-index for PM2.5 is determined via piecewise linear interpolation between established regulatory breakpoints:

AQI = [ (I_high - I_low) / (BP_high - BP_low) ] × (C_PM2.5 - BP_low) + I_low

🛰️ Satellite Fire Detection & FRP Physics

Thermal anomalies are detected in the 3.9 µm and 11 µm mid-infrared channels aboard NASA's S-NPP / NOAA-20 VIIRS instruments (375m spatial resolution). Fire Radiative Power (FRP) in megawatts (MW) measures the instantaneous radiant heat energy output, which correlates directly with crop biomass combustion rates ($\sim 0.368 \pm 0.015 \text{ kg/MJ}$).
